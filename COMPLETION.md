# Oath of the Rose — completion report

A playable browser RPG, built 2026-09-26 by Grok. The single file is `oath-of-the-rose-grok.html`. Open it in Chrome. No server and no network are required.

The source is the NES campaign of Final Fantasy II (the 1988 rebellion story). This is not that game and it does not use Square Enix's title, sprites, maps, or music. Names of people and places follow the established English script. Notes from the guides are in `docs/guides/`. The coverage map is `docs/coverage-checklist.json`.

## What is the source

Use-based growth instead of experience levels. Attack, Magic, Item, and Flee, resolved in agility order. Front and back rows, bows from the back, a weapon in each hand or a weapon and a shield. The fifteen keywords, learned before they can be asked. The walk from the opening loss through Altair, Fynn, Semitt Falls, the snow, Kashuan, the Dreadnought, the Leviathan, the false princess, Castle Fynn, Deist, Mysidia, the Cyclone, Castle Palamecia, the Jade Passage, and Pandaemonium. Leon is the Dark Knight on the Dreadnought and is not that knight at the end.

## What was added

- The Gatrea courier errand and the Rebel Armband.
- Heron Glade, a chocobo trail south of Kashuan with a small cache.
- An Alarm Clock that cures sleep. A hit still wakes a sleeper, as in the original.
- A practice post in Altair, and a shelf of captured tomes so status magic can be learned before the late shops. The spells themselves are from the guides; selling or finding them this early is not.
- Drill Imps on the road and an imperial Executioner on the Fynn patrol, used as a real wipe so Game Over can be continued.
- Island harbors are shoals. The airship will not set down there, so the party's ship is what opens Deist, Mysidia, and the tropical island even after Cid lends the airship. The airship is what crosses the mountains into Palamecia.

## What player complaints changed

The objective stays on screen. Growth is announced and stats do not fall. You do not have to attack your own party. Ordinary enemies can always be fled. Encounters are less frequent, and a chocobo or the airship rides quiet. Dungeons are corridors with chests and a boss, not empty rooms that ambush every step. Save works in towns and on the world map. Cure's rank lists the ailments it can clean. Battle commands are large enough for a thumb. Mute, volume, a fullscreen wake lock, and reduced effects are in Options.

## How to play

New Game. On the Fynn road, choose Fight. You are meant to lose. In Altair, learn Wild Rose and say it to Hilda. The objective line is the route. Talk, Ask, the pad, and Menu are enough on a phone. Keyboard, mouse, and a standard gamepad do the same things.
