# What players asked the remaster to fix

Drawn from public discussion of the 1988 game, not treated as canon:

- The keyword list feels like a reading test. Show the objective, and only offer words the player has actually learned.
- Growth is opaque and slow, and players hurt their own party because that was the efficient trick. Report every gain. Do not drop stats. Do not require attacking allies.
- Random encounters are constant, and some rooms are empty ambushes. Keep encounters, lower the rate, let a chocobo or the airship travel quietly, and make dungeons corridors with a purpose.
- Fleeing a hopeless fight should work against ordinary enemies.
- Rows, turn order, and what a Cure rank actually cleanses should be visible.
- Saving should be possible on the world map and in towns.
- Touch controls need large commands. Keyboard cannot be required.
