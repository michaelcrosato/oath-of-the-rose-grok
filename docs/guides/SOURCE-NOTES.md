# NES Final Fantasy II — working notes

Compiled 2026-09-26 for Oath of the Rose. These are factual notes from public guides, not a reproduction of any walkthrough's prose. Sources:

- StrategyWiki, Final Fantasy II walkthrough chapter table and mission pages: https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_II/Walkthrough
- StrategyWiki items, weapons, armor, white magic, gameplay: https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_II
- RPG Site step order (Pixel Remaster, same plot spine): https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/11551-final-fantasy-ii-walkthrough-where-to-go-missable-bestiary-entries-and-chests-step-by-step-guide
- IGN spell list (English names): https://www.ign.com/wikis/final-fantasy-ii/Spells

The Famicom game has no experience levels. Weapon and spell ranks rise by use. HP rises when a character loses a lot of HP in a battle. MP rises when they spend a lot of MP. Stats rise from the actions that train them. Characters equip a weapon in each hand or a weapon and a shield. Formation puts people in a front or back row. Commands are Attack, Magic, Items, and Flee, chosen with unlimited time, then resolved by agility. Keywords are learned from people and can only be asked once learned.

## Required plot order (NES / early remakes, English names)

1. Outside Fynn the party loses to imperial soldiers and wakes in Altair. Leon is missing.
2. Wild Rose is the rebel password. Firion, Maria, and Guy join Princess Hilda only after they learn it and use it.
3. Scott dies in occupied Fynn. He speaks of Mythril and the Dreadnought. Minwu joins.
4. Paloom's boat reaches Poft. Cid talks about the airship. Josef in Salamand joins if asked about Mythril. His canoe reaches Semitt Falls.
5. Semitt Falls: free Nelly, Paul opens the mythril vault, the mythril is forged into arms.
6. Bafsk Cave hides the Dreadnought. A pass gets the party in. The ship launches.
7. The snowcraft crosses the snowfield. Borghen is fought in the Snow Cavern. The Goddess's Bell is there. Josef dies holding the boulder.
8. The bell opens Kashuan Keep. Gordon joins. Egil's Torch and the words Ekmet Teloess make Sunfire.
9. Sunfire destroys the Dreadnought. Leon appears as the Dark Knight.
10. Leila sails with the party. The Leviathan swallows the ship. A ship is recovered.
11. The Coliseum's "princess" is the Lamia Queen. The real Hilda is in Castle Fynn. Gordon helps. Fynn is freed. Paul opens a way in.
12. Castle Deist and Deist Cavern: the dragoons are gone, the last wyvern remains, Ricard joins.
13. Mysidia, the Mask, the White Mask, the Black Mask, the Cave of Mysidia, and Mysidian Tower. Minwu dies unsealing the Ultima Tome. The survivors can learn Ultima.
14. The Cyclone strikes. Ricard dies there and leaves the party.
15. The Emperor of Palamecia is defeated in Castle Palamecia. He returns from hell. The Jade Passage leads to Pandaemonium, where he is defeated again. Leon is no longer the Dark Knight opposing the party.

## Places

Altair, Gatrea, Paloom, Poft, Salamand, Bafsk, Fynn, Mysidia, the chocobo forest south of Kashuan, Castle Fynn, Semitt Falls, Bafsk Cave, Snow Cavern, Kashuan Keep, the Dreadnought, Castle Deist, Deist Cavern, the Coliseum, Tropical Island, the Leviathan, Cave of Mysidia, Mysidian Tower, the Cyclone, Castle Palamecia, Jade Passage, Pandaemonium.

Travel: on foot, by canoe (rivers), by ship (the sea), by snowcraft (snow), by Cid's airship, and by chocobo. A chocobo is fast and does not draw random encounters. It will not enter towns or dungeons. Dismounting dismisses it. Inns restore the living for gil. Shops sell weapons, armor, items, and spell tomes. Sanctuaries raise the dead for gil.

## Key terms

Wild Rose, Mythril, Dreadnought, Airship, Sunfire, Goddess's Bell, Dragoons, Wyverns, Mysidia, Mask, Ekmet Teloess, Cyclone, Palamecia, Ultima Tome, Jade Passage.

## Spells named in the guides

White: Cure, Life, Basuna, Esuna, Blink, Protect, Shell, Barrier, Wall, Silence, Mini, Fog, Dispel, Slow, Sap, Fear, Teleport, Holy, Swap, Ultima.

Black: Fire, Ice, Bolt, Poison, Sleep, Blind, Stun, Toad, Break, Death, Drain, Osmose, Flare, Warp, Aero, Berserk, Haste, Curse, Scourge, Aura, Charm.

## Status the route must support

Poison, sleep, silence, mini, toad, stone, and death, cured by the matching items (Antidote, Echo Screen, Mallet, Maiden's Kiss, Gold Needle, Phoenix Down) or by cure magic once its rank is high enough. Sleep is also broken by a solid hit in the source game.

Not in this project, because they are not in the 1988 campaign: Soul of Rebirth, the Arcane Labyrinth, and Machanon.
