# The Last Humming Tree

## Quest title

The Last Humming Tree

## Existing-world touchpoint

Gatrea, the town already on the grass at world (17, 18), east of Fynn and west of the road toward Poft. It already has the Gatrea Inn (20 gil), a sanctuary, and the leather-and-iron counter that sells a broadsword, hand axe, shortbow, knife, leather armor, leather cap, and buckler. The orchard is the south hedge of that same town, reached by a gate inside Gatrea, not by a new mark on the world map.

This is not the courier errand. The courier, when he is still in the hall, is a different person with a different job. This quest does not speak to him, does not take his place, and does not use his tile.

## Premise

Gatrea's orchard is in blossom and almost silent. Every fork that used to hold a straw skep is empty except one. That tree, the Boundary Apple, is an old marker growing out of a stone cistern, and its high knot is still full of bees. The town says the hive moved, as if the insects had held a meeting and chosen a better house. The rows are green. They will not set fruit without the workers.

The hive did not move. On a windy night someone smoked the lower trees with green applewood, scraped the combs outward, and carried the skeps to a hollow in the west wall. He left the Boundary Apple because the knot is too narrow to rob quickly and the trunk is in sight of the inn's back window at dawn. The few bees still crossing the empty rows are not scouts of a new kingdom. They are foragers from the stolen colonies, correcting toward the only queen he could not carry.

This is not the courier errand. No paper changes hands, and nobody is asked to leave Gatrea.

## Why the player becomes involved

Sella Holt stops the party on the south lane because the hum can be heard from the path and the rest of the orchard cannot. She wants the rows walked before the town decides the last tree is bad luck and smokes it too. She does not ask for a password, a rank, or proof that anyone has joined a rebellion. Outsiders suit her: they are not her neighbor, and they are not yet bored of the story the way Gatrea is.

Anyone who can enter Gatrea can take the work. It does not wait on a story phase. If Guy is present and alive he can put the flight line in fewer words ("Bees go home. Not away."), but the quest does not require him or any other named member of the party. Sella can see the line herself once someone stands in the rows at midmorning.

## Key NPCs

- **Sella Holt**, orchardist. She counts trees the way other people count coins. Her first guess is blight, and her second guess is that soldiers salted the blossom from the direction of Fynn. Both guesses are wrong, and she would rather either of them were right than the third possibility, which is the man who mends her wall. She never leaves the orchard gate or the rows.
- **Bram Holl**, wall-mender. He keeps a bee-smoker, a pot of reduced windfall, and a quiet voice. He is not a soldier and not a spy. A tinker who does not ask which tree pays him for wax, and his own row failed. He will not draw a weapon. If the party does, he sets the smoker down and waits. He is not an encounter.
- **Odder Pim**, dawn baker. He fires the inn's oven before the innkeeper is up, and he is not that innkeeper. He stands once at the town side of the orchard gate. The night smoke walked at knee height and smelled of green applewood and scorched honey, not of flour. After he has said that, he has nothing else to sell and nothing else to add.

None of these three replace the innkeeper, the shopkeeper, the priest, or the courier.

## New locations or spaces

All of this is one sub-map, `gatrea-orchard`, entered from a south gate on Gatrea's town map and exited back to that map. It is not a new `LOCATIONS` entry, not a dock, and not a place Travel lists on its own. Encounter rate is zero. A chocobo already refuses Gatrea; it never reaches this gate.

- **The rows.** A short field of apple trees. Empty forks show soot on the lip of the knot and wax scraped outward. The bark was not burned. The combs were pulled.
- **The ditch.** Irrigation along the low side. The water carries a skin of boiled windfall, sweet and wrong. It is bait residue, not a poison laid for the town.
- **The Boundary Apple and its cistern.** The one humming tree. The cistern under the roots is stone and is not fed by the ditch, so the water stays clean. The queen is in the high knot. She is an animal in a tree, not a monster and not a boss tile.
- **The west-wall hollow.** A dead-end pocket in the orchard wall, wide enough for three skeps and a man. No stairs, no chest that belongs to any other map, no second exit into the grass.

## Quest progression / major beats

**Beginning.** In Gatrea, the south lane carries a single hum. Sella is at the orchard gate. She names the silence: every skep is gone except the colony in the Boundary Apple, and she wants to know why the hive moved before someone calls the last tree cursed. She offers no errand out of town. Accepting opens the gate. Refusing leaves her there, and the campaign is unchanged.

**Escalation.** The rows are a reading, not a fight. Empty knots were smoked and scraped, not abandoned from the inside. The ditch is sugared with boiled windfall. The cistern is clean, and the high knot is still occupied. At midmorning, workers stitch a straight flight from the empty forks to the Boundary Apple, and a second, shorter stitch from the west wall back to that same tree. Odder, at the gate, separates that night's smoke from his oven: green wood, knee-high, honey scorched. Sella's theory about soldiers does not survive the smell. Nothing here is a keyword, and asking one gets the same answer these people give to any word they do not know: nothing new.

**Climax.** Following the wall stitch on foot ends at the hollow. Three stolen skeps are alive and restless, orienting on the queen Bram could not take. Bram is there with the smoker still in his hands. He explains the failed row, the tinker, and the night he needed the town to say the bees had left. The party chooses here, in the hollow, with the orchard still on every side. No battle starts. The smoker, used slowly, is what lets skeps be lifted without a sting; the intended path never requires one.

**Resolution.** The chosen settlement is carried out on this map and reported, or not reported, to Sella at the gate. The party walks back into Gatrea. The inn, the sanctuary, the shops, and the hall are as they were. Only the orchard's dialogue and one local flag change.

## Important player choices

The quest has one deciding choice, made in the west-wall hollow once the skeps and Bram are both in front of the party. It has three resolutions, written again in the next section.

1. **Return the skeps and name him.** Match each skep to the scraped fork it came from. The soot lips fit. Tell Sella it was Bram. The rows are restocked before the party leaves the map.
2. **Split the work.** Two skeps go back to the rows. One stays in the hollow, in the open, and Bram owes Sella a third of its wax on Gatrea's own market days. Straw markers teach the flight line to fork. Nobody travels to collect the debt. The party does not become the collector.
3. **Take the wax and leave it unspoken.** Lift two capped combs. Do not move the skeps and do not tell Sella. The hollow keeps its theft, the rows stay empty, and the Boundary Apple keeps humming alone.

Smaller looks (tasting the ditch, asking Odder, listening with Guy if he is there) only confirm the same facts. They do not branch the quest. Drawing on Bram does not open a fight and does not count as a fourth ending. He is not an encounter. Putting a weapon away returns to the same three choices.

## Possible resolutions

**Restored.** The skeps are back in the forks, Bram is named, and he stays in Gatrea as a wall-mender who no longer keeps bees. The next time the party enters the orchard, the rows hum and the hollow is bare. Sella is not grateful in a soft way. She is accurate. She pays what the work was worth and does not ask the party to punish him. No one is killed, exiled onto the world map, or handed to any authority outside town.

**Split.** The Boundary Apple and two forks hum for Sella. The hollow hums for Bram, where people can see it. The agreement is thin and local: a third of the wax, paid inside Gatrea, with no courier and no road. Speaking to either of them afterward gets a short, cold confirmation that the bargain is holding. It does not open a second quest.

**Unspoken.** The party leaves with two capped combs. Sella, if asked, still believes the hive chose one tree. That belief is the sharp residue: the green rows keep lying. If the party tells her later, Bram has already pushed the remaining skeps deeper into the wall. The quest does not become a search, does not offer a second reward, and does not leave Gatrea. The Boundary Apple still hums. The rows do not.

Ignoring the gate, or walking away before the hollow, is not a resolution. Sella remains. Nothing else in the world notices.

## Gameplay opportunities

The activity is attention. The player walks a small map with no random encounters and reads three signs: scraped wax and soot on empty knots, sweet film on the ditch against clean water in the cistern, and a flight line that is only visible if they stand in the rows at midmorning. The line is a path, not a timer that can be failed. Missing it means Odder and the soot are still enough to find the wall; the flight is the proof, not a lock.

Moving skeps is a placement, not a battle. Each stolen hive fits one scraped fork. The smoker Bram already lit keeps the bees from rising. That is the whole climax mechanic.

A sting exists only if the player yanks a comb loose before the choice, against the clear handling. It applies ordinary poison to that character and does not start a battle. Antidote cures it. Cure cures poison once that spell's rank already can, and Basuna can as well, under rules the game already uses. Resting at the Gatrea Inn already clears ordinary statuses on the living. None of this is required to finish, and none of it changes how sleep works. Keeper's Honey, below, cures poison and restores a little HP. It does not cure sleep, and it is not a substitute for a hit waking a sleeper.

No keyword is taught. No weapon rank is required. Flee, rows, and the practice post are untouched.

## Worldbuilding revealed

Gatrea feeds itself. From this hedge the war is a direction, Fynn's direction, and people reach for it too quickly. Sella's story about salted blossom is what a town says when the empire is the weather. The actual wound is a neighbor stealing a living ahead of a lean season, and using that weather as cover. The orchard is the occupation as it looks when no patrol is in the frame: still green, and useless.

Bees here are kept in straw skeps set in forks. The Boundary Apple is older than the inn. Its roots drink from a cistern the ditch cannot reach, which is why bait in the water never touched it. Smoke used in daylight, with care, moves a colony. The same smoke at night, followed by a scraped knot, is theft. "The hive moved" is the sentence that protects the thief.

The hornets that already rise from grass, forest, road, and the odd stretch of sea are wild monsters. They are not this orchard's workers, and they do not come when the Boundary Apple is disturbed. The Queen Bee listed among the bestiary entries is that monster, not the queen in the knot. This quest does not place her, rename her, or fight her.

Heron Glade stays what it is: a chocobo trail south of Kashuan, with its own small cache, far from this hedge. Nothing in the orchard is a chocobo, a cache on that trail, or a reason to go there.

## Rewards

Rewards are local consumables and a little gil. They are not equipment, not evasion, and not a mark of anyone's service.

- **Restored:** 80 gil and three jars of Keeper's Honey.
- **Split:** 40 gil and one jar. The wax-tithe is Bram's debt to Sella. The party is not paid again for it.
- **Unspoken:** the two capped combs, each usable as one Keeper's Honey or sold for 40 gil. Sella pays nothing.

Keeper's Honey is a new consumable, suggested id `keepers-honey`, used from the menu. It restores 40 HP and cures poison. It is not sold in any existing shop, not a key item, and not a tome. It does not cure sleep and does not interact with the Alarm Clock, which remains the sleep-cure already sold in Altair, Paloom, and Fynn.

## Collision risks

- The courier stands in Gatrea's hall until his own errand is done, and his lines must stay exactly as they are, including the refusal before the party is trusted and the later line that Gatrea owes them for a dispatch. This quest uses a different map and must not occupy `TOWN_SPOTS.hall`, must not replace `gatrea-courier`, and must not set `courierStarted` or `courierComplete`.
- Do not hand the player a sealed dispatch, send them to Altair, speak to the quartermaster, or grant the Rebel Armband. Those belong only to the existing errand.
- Do not add a world-map location. A new town, cave, or road would risk the reach map and would tempt a finish somewhere else. The sub-map's `locationId` can remain Gatrea so it is obviously the same town; its exit returns to Gatrea's town map, not to the world.
- Do not spawn `hornet` or `queen-bee` here, and do not give the orchard a boss. Queen Bee stays an unused bestiary monster unless some other, separate placement already owns her. Hornet stays a wild encounter on the tables that already list her.
- Do not put a chest on this map that uses an existing chest id, and do not hide mythril, the bell, a mask, Sunfire, or any other key item in a skep.
- Do not change inn rest, sanctuary price, Cure's rank list, or the Alarm Clock. A sting, if it happens at all, uses poison that those systems already know how to clear.
- Do not contradict Heron Glade. It remains the chocobo trail south of Kashuan with a small cache. This orchard is not that glade, has no chocobo, and is not a reason the trail exists.
- Odder must not take the innkeeper's tile or dialogue. Sella and Bram must not take the shopkeeper, the priest, or the courier.
- Suggested local flags only: something in the shape of `beesHollowFound` and `beesResolution` (`restored`, `split`, or `unspoken`). No shared story flag.
- Other side quests may also invent consumables. `keepers-honey` should stay unique to this draft and should not be wired into the courier's reward or into any shop table that already exists.

## Why the quest remains self-contained

It starts at Gatrea's south gate and ends in Gatrea's orchard. The player is never sent to another town, another dungeon, or another side quest to finish. This is not the courier errand: that errand remains a sealed dispatch carried to Altair's quartermaster for gil and a Rebel Armband, and this quest never touches it, whether the courier is still in the hall, already paid, or not yet willing to talk.

Any of the three resolutions, or no resolution at all, leaves the campaign, the keywords, the required places, Heron Glade, and the Alarm Clock as they are. Gatrea keeps its inn, its sanctuary, and its shops. The only thing that changes is whether the rows hum, whether the hollow is allowed to hum in the open, or whether one tree goes on carrying a theft the town has agreed to call a choice the bees made.
