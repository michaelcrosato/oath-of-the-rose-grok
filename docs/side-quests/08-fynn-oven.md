## Quest title

The Last Firing

## Existing-world touchpoint

Fynn, the town at world (12, 16) whose entry map is `fynn`. It is already a town interior: sixteen by twelve, encounter rate 0, with the inn, shop, and sanctuary along the north wall and the town exit on the south. Castle Fynn is a different place and is not entered. The quest uses the town the player can already walk into any time after the opening, including the occupied visit in the `to-fynn` errand and any later return. Fynn's liberation is not a gate and is not an outcome.

Marta Quill stands on the south lane of that map, at (12, 8), away from the inn (3, 2), the shop (6, 2), the priest (9, 2), and the hall (4, 6). Her door is the tile beside her, (13, 8). Nothing in her lane points at the hall.

## Premise

Marta still keeps the bakehouse tally for three neighbors the occupation marched out of Fynn at dawn: Ilen, at the blue shutter; Tam, the miller's boy; Lina, the cooper's wife. Her brother Cal went with them as a porter. He was the one who lifted the oven's iron door. The last sack from Tam's mill is hidden under the ash bench, because a chalk-man with a list counts flour in the lane. The only wood left is the slats of the bread-trough Cal cracked when he set it down to join the column.

She will light the oven once more. Then she will brick the mouth with those nails and with stones from the lane. She will not bake for the garrison, and she will not keep a fire that calls them. The quest is that firing, and the question of where the loaves go. It is not a rescue and not a battle.

## Why the player becomes involved

The player is already in Fynn. The south lane smells of cold ash. Marta has the iron door half-raised on a peel and cannot hold it; Cal did that. She asks whoever is standing there to take the weight so the hidden sack is not spilled in the lane for the chalk-man. She does not ask their names, does not know them, and does not care whether they are armed so long as steel stays off her table. If Guy is in the party she may nod at him because he is large. Anyone can hold the door. The peel on the wall is the lever if no one wants to trust their hands.

She is not recruiting. Walking away leaves her as she was, door half-up or not, and the main errand in Fynn is untouched.

## Key NPCs

- **Marta Quill**, baker of Fynn's south lane. The only new person who speaks. Middle-aged, flour in the cuff of a dress she has stopped washing white, voice quiet and exact. She names people by what they ate. She will not say Cal is dead. She says the cot over the oven is his until the bread he liked is gone. She does not know Scott, has not been into the hall, and does not keep passwords.
- **Remembered, not present:** Cal Quill, her brother, who lifted the door and broke the trough. Ilen, who took the burnt heel because her molars were gone; the boy who lived with her left a wooden spoon in the ash the morning the column formed, and went with her. Tam, two dots on the tally, who brought the last sack at night and was taken at the mill before dawn because he knew where the stores were. Lina, whose loaf was scored with a cooper's hoop; she went out still wearing flour on her cuff, following the cooper. None of them has a sprite, a map, or a rescue.
- **A lane soldier, conditional and unnamed.** Only if smoke slips the oven-room. He comes to the doorway, is shown a pan of spoiled scrap, and leaves. He is not Gottos, not Borghen, not the road executioner, has no loot, and cannot be fought. After Fynn is free by the main plot, this beat is a loose shutter instead of a soldier. Same result.

The innkeeper, shopkeeper, and priest stay on their service lines. They are not quest givers, recipients the player must find, or people Marta needs the party to convince.

## New locations or spaces

One interior only: the **oven-room** (`fynn-oven`), reached by the door at (13, 8) on the Fynn map and left by the same door. It is not a world location, not a travel entry, and not a dungeon. One room, encounter rate 0, no stairs, no second floor, no cellar. The cot, the tally, and the oven are all in that room.

Props, all in the room: the iron door of the oven; the ash bench and the spoon in the ash; the flour sack; the salt pig, with a last crust of salt in the bottom; the trough slats; Cal's three words chalked inside the door, "Damper. Slats. Turn."; the tally board (heel-notch, two dots, a hoop); three cloths; the match; a loose brick and the trough nails, set where she can reach them after the draw.

Three narrow ledges in the west wall look onto shut lane shutters. They are interact points in this room, not doors to new maps. The threshold board is the step the player is already standing on when they face Marta.

Saving treats `fynn-oven` as part of Fynn. The player is still in town.

## Quest progression / major beats

**Beginning.** Talk to Marta in Fynn. She will not light anything until someone holds the iron door. Agreeing sets the visit in motion and moves the party into the oven-room. She shows the tally and names Ilen, Tam, and Lina once, by their marks, not as a speech. Refusing, or never speaking to her, does nothing to the rest of the game. She remains at (12, 8) from the first time Fynn can be entered until the mouth is bricked, including after the castle changes hands.

**Escalation.** The work in the room, in any order, all of it required before the match will catch:

- Clear the ash bench. The spoon comes up. Marta says whose it was, in one line, and puts it on the tally ledge. It is not loot yet.
- Pull the sack into the open, inside the room only.
- Stack the trough slats in the mouth.
- Lift the door and prop it. The peel is the lever.
- She upends the salt pig into the dough herself. There is no argument about the salt. The pig is empty afterward, and that is the point: this dough does not have a next time.

If the player leaves mid-work, the room keeps its state. On the lane she says the door is up and the sack must not pass the chalk-man. No timer and no fail.

**Climax.** The last firing. She strikes the match only after the dough is in. Three tending beats follow the chalk inside the door: Damper, then Slats, then Turn. Each beat is a field choice with those three words. Follow the chalk and the crust comes even, and the lane stays quiet. Choose another order and smoke slips once: the unnamed soldier (or, after the main plot has freed Fynn, the loose shutter) interrupts, Marta shows the pan of scrap she keeps for this, and the interruption ends. One loaf cracks. It is still a loaf. There is no battle, no game over, and no way to lose the dough. She draws what the sack made: three loaves on the peel, plus the heel she always cuts for Ilen, which is not a fourth share.

Then she asks, once, where they go. She will not brick the mouth until the player answers, but she will not light the oven again if they walk off to think. The loaves wait in the room, warm and then cool. Cool loaves still count.

**Resolution.** The player carries out the answer below. When they come back to the doorway she sets the loose brick and the trough nails herself. The party may watch. They do not get a brick-laying minigame. The mouth is a brick face from then on. The tally stays. Marta returns to (12, 8) with a short line that matches the answer, and never offers another firing. The oven-room can still be entered. Nothing in it starts combat.

## Important player choices

The only branching choice is Marta's question after the draw: **where do the loaves go?**

- **The shut sills.** Set one loaf on each west ledge, for Ilen, Tam, and Lina, in the cloths that match the tally marks. The heel stays on Ilen's ledge with her loaf. Marta keeps nothing.
- **The ones who stayed.** Leave all three on the threshold board in her own doorway, cut, uncovered, for whoever still sleeps on this lane. She keeps the heel back only long enough to put it in the player's hands, because Ilen is not here to claim it and she will not eat Ilen's piece.
- **The column's road.** Wrap all three and the heel in one cloth and use the bundle on Fynn's existing town-exit tile (the south exit of map `fynn`). Not the world map, not the outskirts, not the road encounter. The stone is where that dawn column stood while names were chalked.

Walking away before the question, or refusing the door at the start, is not a resolution. The quest stays open and Fynn is unchanged. Botching the damper is not a choice with its own ending; it only changes whether one loaf is cracked and whether the doorway is interrupted.

No keyword is asked or taught. Saying Wild Rose, or any other learned word, gets a blank look and the same request about the door.

## Possible resolutions

All three resolutions brick the oven, set a done flag, leave Marta in Fynn, change no story phase, and can be reached without combat. The player cannot take more than one.

- **The shut sills.** The ledges hold the loaves and Ilen's heel. Doors on the lane stay shut. Marta bricks the mouth and says the tally can keep them until someone who remembers is hungry, or until mice. If the player looks again later, including after the main plot frees Fynn, the cloths are empty and the people are not back. She does not pretend to know who ate. She gives the player the spoon off the ledge.
- **The ones who stayed.** The board at her threshold is for the living lane, not the garrison: she names no heroes, only that the night-washer who already sleeps by the sanctuary and the scullion who never comes out of the inn will take a share when they pass, and she will not stand over them. Those two are not new NPCs and are not visited. She puts Ilen's heel in the player's pack and will not discuss it. The board is bare the next time the room is entered. The bricks go in the same hour.
- **The column's road.** The bundle left on the town-exit stone is gone the next time the player speaks to Marta. In its place on the stone: a twist of ordinary mill-twine and a wooden button that is not a uniform button. She has already gone and looked. She does not know if a marched neighbor, a soldier, or a thief took the bread. She gives the player the twine and the forty gil that were in the salt pig, which she had been keeping for Cal's boots. The bricks go in. She stops watching the stone.

There is no resolution in which the neighbors return, the oven stays in service, the garrison is fought, or the castle changes hands.

## Gameplay opportunities

- A town errand with no required combat and no random encounters. Fynn's rate is already 0; the oven-room matches it. Flee, rows, and weapon ranks are not involved.
- Field verbs the game already uses: talk to Marta, move between two maps, examine and use the props, choose among Damper, Slats, and Turn. No new battle command and no new mode.
- The firing is a short order puzzle whose failure state is smoke and a cracked loaf, cleaned up by dialogue. It can be done by a party of any composition. No guest, no dead ally, and no specific party member is required.
- Placement is carrying three quest loaves (`warm-loaf`, not sellable, not usable in battle) to ledges, to the threshold, or to the town exit tile. Leaving them on the ground anywhere else does not count. Dropping them in the world, or selling, is not offered.
- The smoke soldier is a talk interruption with one exit line. Drawing a weapon in that doorway makes Marta step between; the soldier still leaves; no encounter id is started. After `fynnLiberated` is already true from the main plot, skip the soldier sprite and use the shutter line. The quest must not read that flag in order to be completable. It only swaps a sentence.
- One visit is enough. Mid-quest progress is a few boolean flags on the existing flag record (`ovenDoorUp`, `ovenLaid`, `ovenFired`, `ovenSmoke`, `ovenSills`, `ovenBoard`, `ovenRoad`). None of those keys exist today.
- Rewards are consumables weaker than the Hi-Potions Fynn already sells, plus a key item with examine text, or a trivial gil pile. They do not teach spells, do not raise stats, and do not open shops.
- The objective line of the main plot is not replaced. A local log note is enough ("Marta is waiting on the south lane" / "The oven mouth is brick").

## Worldbuilding revealed

Occupation in Fynn, at the size of one kitchen. The empire did not have to burn the bakehouse. A chalk-man walked the south lane at dawn, marked who could carry, and walked them out with sacks: Ilen with her blanket, Tam from the mill, Lina with flour still on her cuff, Cal as a porter. The inn, the shop, and the sanctuary stayed open, which is why a player can already buy a potion in occupied Fynn. Commerce for people who can pay is not the same as a lane of shut shutters.

Bread was Marta's census. The tally board is heel-notch, two dots, and a hoop, and she still speaks of those marks in the present tense. Salt was a crust in a pig, not a cargo. Wood was a broken trough. The oven is older than the garrison's use of the town, and bricking it is her decision, not an order. She would rather have no fire than a fire the chalk-man can smell.

What the quest refuses to reveal is the rest of the war. She does not know why the column wanted porters, what the flour was taken for, or who holds the castle. If the banners over Fynn have already changed when the player finally helps her, she has one line for it: the lane did not give her neighbors back. She does not cheer, and she does not ask the party to finish anything.

## Rewards

Completion always bricks the oven and leaves the tally to be read. No resolution gives equipment, a keyword, a guest, or a shop discount. The Fynn inn stays 60 gil. The Fynn shelves stay as they are.

- **The shut sills.** `ilen-spoon`, a key item, not sellable, not equippable. Examine text is Marta's line about Ilen's heel and the boy's spoon. No stat.
- **The ones who stayed.** One `burnt-heel`. Consumable, single ally, heal 40. Weaker than a Potion. Not sold, not restocked. It is Ilen's piece, and using it is ordinary inventory use, with no scene.
- **The column's road.** `mill-twine`, a key item, examine-only, plus 40 gil from the salt pig.

Taking the spoon, the heel, or the gil before the matching placement is not allowed. Nothing here stacks into a second reward if the player later changes their mind; the mouth is already brick.

## Collision risks

- **Scott.** Until the player talks to him, he stands at the hall tile (4, 6) on the same map. Marta is at (12, 8). Her door, her path, and her lines must not send the player to the hall, mention a prince, a wound, or a ring, or share his sprite. Finishing or ignoring her quest must not set `scottDead`, give `ring`, or teach Mythril or Dreadnought. She has no line about him if he is already dead.
- **Castle plot on the other map.** Castle Fynn, Gottos, the Lamia Queen, Hilda's cell, Paul's gate, and the dungeon boss are `castle-fynn-*` and the coliseum. This quest never travels there, never sets `paulFynnGate`, `gottosDefeated`, `hildaRescued`, or `fynnLiberated`, and never starts those encounters.
- **Town services.** Do not hook the loaf delivery through the innkeeper, shopkeeper, or priest. Their talk, rest, prices, and sanctuary lines stay as they are. The "ones who stayed" are served by the threshold board, off-screen.
- **Exit tile.** The bundle goes on map `fynn`'s existing south exit. Using it there must not run `leaveMap`, must not dump the party on the world map, and must not load the outskirts ambush.
- **Combat leftovers.** Do not add an encounter rate to `fynn` or `fynn-oven`, and do not reuse the Fynn-road executioner, Drill Imps, or any boss id. The smoke soldier has no encounter id.
- **Items and words.** Do not reuse `ring`, `alarm-clock`, `rebel-armband`, `sealed-dispatch`, mythril, Sunfire, the bell, or the fifteen keywords. Do not add a sixteenth keyword. New ids are only `warm-loaf`, `ilen-spoon`, `burnt-heel`, and `mill-twine`.
- **Names.** Marta, Cal, Ilen, Tam, and Lina are not in the current cast. Do not give them the jobs or the fates of Nelly, Paul, Cid, Josef, Borghen, or the Altair quartermaster. The chalk-man is not that quartermaster.
- **Save and travel.** `fynn-oven` must not appear in the world travel list. If it is omitted from the town save allowlist, the player can lose a firing to a reload they expected to be legal. Treat the room as Fynn for saving.
- **Party identity.** Do not make the four youths Marta's family, do not name their parents, and do not retell the opening loss as her story. She does not recognize them.

## Why the quest remains self-contained

It starts and ends on the Fynn town map plus one oven-room. The only actor it adds is Marta. The neighbors are three marks on a board and a spoon in the ash. The player's whole task is to hold a door, tend one fire, and choose which shut place gets the bread. When the brick is in, her dialogue goes quiet and no later town, dungeon, guest, or ending checks the flags.

Ignoring her changes nothing that the main plot checks. Completing her changes nothing those checks look for. The castle is freed, or not yet freed, by the same scenes as always. Scott still dies only if the player speaks to Scott, and he does not die in her kitchen. The loaves do not arm the rebellion, open a road, or come back as a second quest.
